Overview

Hold the line, or lose the map

Battlespace is a multiplayer first-person shooter about coordinated infantry combat at scale. Two teams contest a contiguous front line across large maps, capturing and holding objectives while managing limited reinforcements, supply and vehicle assets.

Players are organised into squads under a squad leader, with a commander coordinating the wider effort. Voice communication is a core mechanic rather than an add-on: local proximity chat, squad channels and a command net all carry different information, and squads that use them well beat squads that do not.

Combat is deliberately unforgiving. Time-to-kill is short, there is no health regeneration in the field, and a downed player depends on a teammate to revive them. Suppression, cover, sightlines and the direction you approach from matter more than raw aim. Winning a fight is usually a question of getting there first with the right people.

Features

What you actually play

01

Large-scale infantry combat

{{ site.game.players }} on persistent front lines, fought over open terrain, treelines and built-up areas rather than corridor arenas.

02

Squad-based command

Players group into squads under a squad leader. Leaders place rally points and forward positions; the commander allocates assets across the map.

03

Positional voice comms

Proximity, squad and command voice channels. Hearing an enemy squad through a wall is as useful as seeing them.

04

Logistics and supply

Ammunition, construction and fortifications are hauled forward by players. A cut supply line loses ground without a shot fired.

05

Combined arms

Light vehicles, transports and crew-served weapons support infantry. Armour without infantry cover does not last long.

06

Role loadouts

Rifleman, medic, automatic rifleman, marksman, engineer and anti-tank roles, limited per squad so team composition is a real decision.
