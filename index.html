Who We Are

An independent studio that ships its own games

{% include entity-line.html %} Founded in {{ site.company.founded }}, we handle every stage of our titles in-house — design, engineering, art, live operations, player support and publishing. There is no third-party publisher between us and our players.

01 — Developer

We build the game

Battlespace is developed in-house by Impact Forge LLC using Unreal Engine 5. Gameplay systems, networking, art and audio are all produced by our own team.

02 — Publisher

We publish the game

Impact Forge LLC is also the publisher of record for Battlespace. We own the intellectual property, hold the store relationships, and are the party responsible for distribution, pricing and player-facing terms.

03 — Operator

We run the services

We operate the online services behind Battlespace, including account sign-in, matchmaking and player support, and we are the data controller for the personal information those services process.
