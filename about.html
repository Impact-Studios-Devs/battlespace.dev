How We Work

Principles we hold to

  • Depth over spectacle. Systems that reward learning beat systems that reward reflexes. We would rather a player win because they read the map correctly.
  • Honest communication. We say what is in development and what is not. We do not announce dates we cannot hold, and we do not sell what does not exist yet.
  • Fair monetisation. No pay-to-win. Nothing sold in Battlespace confers a competitive advantage in a match.
  • Respect for player data. We collect what the game needs to run and to be supported, we say exactly what that is, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Accountability. Support requests, privacy requests and legal notices reach the studio directly and are answered by us.
